In this episode, Ethan interviews Mr. Bob White, landscape architect and alumn
of the Harvard Graduate School of Design. They discuss his early life growing
up in the Appalachian mountains, his time in college and graduate school, and
his early career as a landscape architect and his eventual founding of his own
business. From there, they discuss some of the work he has done, including how
his Quaker beliefs have influenced some of his projects. Finally, he discusses
his work on the Franconia Ridge Trail Loop restoration project and how its
intentional and sustainable design protects it from constant traffic and
weather.



Thank you again Mr. White for being on the podcast.
